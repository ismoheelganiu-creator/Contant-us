About the ICT Club

The ICT Club helps students learn about computers, technology, programming, and digital creativity. Members work together to develop useful skills.

The club encourages teamwork, communication, creativity, problem-solving, and responsible use of technology.

Our Objectives

  • Improve students' computer skills.
  • Encourage students to learn programming.
  • Develop creative technology projects.
  • Promote teamwork among club members.
  • Prepare students for ICT Day activities.

Student Project Planning

The ICT Club encourages members to create practical projects that demonstrate their computer and technology skills.

Suggested Projects

  • Personal portfolio website.
  • School event announcement webpage.
  • Student contact form.
  • Simple school information website.
  • Basic computer skills presentation.

Project Requirements

Students should plan their projects, organise their files, test their work, and prepare a short explanation of how each project works.

ICT Club Meeting Rules

  • Arrive on time for meetings.
  • Respect the opinions of other members.
  • Listen carefully when someone is speaking.
  • Use school computers responsibly.
  • Complete assigned tasks before deadlines.
  • Keep the computer laboratory clean.
  • Work together to solve problems.
